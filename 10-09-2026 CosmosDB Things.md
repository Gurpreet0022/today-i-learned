# Cosmos DB things

**Date**: 10-09-2026
1. Choose the right partion key for better performance.
2. Avoid cross partition queries.
3. Choose right consistency level based on your needs.
4. Never choose a low cardinality partition key otherwise you may hit hot partitioning issue.
5. Leave default indexing policy , because cosmos automatically indexes items.
